FAQ

Common questions

Who do you work with?

We work with small and mid-sized businesses, startups preparing to raise capital, and individuals who want a structured financial plan.

How are your fees structured?

Most engagements are fixed-fee, agreed before work begins. Ongoing services such as fractional CFO support are billed as a monthly retainer.

Do you replace my accountant?

Not necessarily. We often work alongside your existing bookkeeper or accountant, adding strategic planning and analysis on top of compliance work.

Can you work with clients remotely?

Yes. We serve clients both in person and remotely using secure document sharing and video meetings.
