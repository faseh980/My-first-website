Does this sound familiar?

Has your business outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools? FI Consultancy implements accounting and ERP systems tailored to your business. We map your processes and choose the right platform for your size, industry and budget. Then we design your chart of accounts, workflows and approvals around how you work. Your data is migrated cleanly, with opening balances fully reconciled. We connect your bank, payroll, e-commerce and payment apps, so data flows automatically. Your team is trained, and we support you through go-live and beyond. Our approach: discover, design, configure and migrate, then train and go live. The result: one system, accurate records, and real-time visibility as you grow. Book a free ERP consultation with FI Consultancy today.
