Overview

Does this sound familiar?

Most growing businesses look at last month's numbers. The best ones look ahead. We build the budgets, forecasts and dashboards that show where your business is going, and give you an experienced CFO to help you act on them.

FAQs

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly

What does a fractional CFO actually do?

A fractional CFO does what a full-time CFO does, including planning, reporting, funding and strategy, but for a set number of days each month.

How many days a month would we need?

Most clients start with two to four days a month and adjust as needs change, for example during a fundraise.

Will you work with our existing accountant?

Yes. We usually work alongside your bookkeeper or accountant and focus on planning, analysis and decision support.

Which tools do you use for dashboards?

We work with the tools you already have, from Excel and Google Sheets to Power BI and cloud accounting platforms.
