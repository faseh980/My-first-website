Overview

Does this sound familiar?

Big transactions happen rarely, so most owners go through them with little experience. We bring structure and independent judgement, from the first valuation and business plan to due diligence, negotiation and completion.

  • You're preparing to raise funding and need an investor-ready model.
  • You've received an offer for your business and don't know what it's worth.
  • You're buying a company and need to know what you're really getting.
  • You need a business plan or feasibility study for a bank or partner.

How we help

Our deals & business advisory services

01

Business valuations

Independent valuations for transactions, fundraising, shareholder agreements, disputes and financial reporting.

02

Financial due diligence

Buy-side and vendor due diligence focused on quality of earnings, cash, debt and the risks that affect price.

03

M&A advisory

Support through the deal: target screening, deal structuring, negotiation support and completion.

04

Fundraising & investor readiness

Financial models, pitch-deck numbers, data rooms and investor Q&A for equity or debt raises.

Business plans & feasibility studies

Bankable business plans and feasibility studies for new projects, expansions and financing applications.

Restructuring & business setup

Company formation, group structuring, and turnaround support for businesses under financial pressure.

FAQs

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly

When should we start preparing for a fundraise or sale?

Ideally six to twelve months ahead. Clean financials and a solid model take time, and early preparation improves valuation.

Do you act for buyers or sellers?

Both. We act for one side in any transaction and check for conflicts of interest before we start.

What valuation methods do you use?

We typically use discounted cash flow, comparable companies and precedent transactions, and explain how each affects the result.
