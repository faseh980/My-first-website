FAQs

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly

When should I get advice before buying or selling a business?

As early as possible. Six to twelve months before a sale gives time to tidy up the numbers and improve the price. When buying, talk to us before you agree terms.

How do you value a business?

We look at the cash the business is expected to generate, what similar businesses sell for and recent comparable deals, then explain clearly how we reached the figure.

What goes into a business plan?

Your goals, market, team and strategy, backed by realistic forecasts of sales, costs, cash flow and funding needs.

Do you work with our lawyers and bank?

Yes. We work alongside your lawyers, bank and other advisers so the process runs smoothly.
