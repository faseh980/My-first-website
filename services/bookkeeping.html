Are your books always a month behind? FI Consultancy keeps your books accurate, up to date and ready for decisions. Day-to-day bookkeeping: sales, purchases, expenses and payroll recorded accurately. Every bank and card account is reconciled each month, so your balances always agree. A structured month-end close, with accruals and adjustments finished within five working days. We work with a wide range of accounting and business software, including the tools you already use. Then every month you receive clear reports: profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow. Our approach is simple: onboard, clean up, automate, then close your books every month. The result: accurate records, on-time reports, and more time to run your business. QuickBooks OnlineCloud accounting

Popular cloud accounting for small and growing businesses, with strong bank feeds and a large app marketplace.

  • New company setup and chart of accounts
  • Migration from desktop or spreadsheets
  • Bank rules, receipt capture and reconciliations
  • Monthly bookkeeping and reporting
XeroCloud accounting

Cloud accounting built for collaboration, with automatic bank feeds and many connected apps.

  • Setup, tracking categories and tax settings
  • Bank feeds and reconciliation rules
  • Inventory, projects and payroll add-ons
  • Monthly bookkeeping and reporting
Zoho BooksCloud accounting

Affordable cloud accounting that connects with the wider Zoho suite, ideal for startups and service businesses.

  • Setup, workflows and approvals
  • Bank feeds and automation
  • Connection with Zoho CRM and Inventory
  • Monthly bookkeeping and reporting
Microsoft Dynamics 365Business software

For businesses running on Microsoft Dynamics 365, we keep the finance side accurate and up to date.

  • General ledger and journals
  • Payables, receivables and bank reconciliation
  • Month-end close and reporting
SAPBusiness software

For companies on SAP, including SAP Business One, we support day-to-day accounting and the month-end close.

  • Transaction posting and reconciliation
  • Intercompany and multi-currency entries
  • Month-end close and reporting
Oracle NetSuiteCloud ERP

For businesses on Oracle NetSuite, we handle bookkeeping, reconciliations and close across entities.

  • Multi-entity bookkeeping
  • Bank and intercompany reconciliation
  • Consolidated monthly reporting

FAQs

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly

Which accounting software do you work with?

We have worked with QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books and Xero, as well as other accounting and business software such as Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP and Oracle NetSuite.

Can you catch up on a backlog?

Yes. We start with a clean-up to bring your books up to date, then move to a regular monthly routine.

How do I send you receipts and invoices?

Through receipt-capture apps, a shared inbox or direct bank feeds, so most of it happens automatically.

How is bookkeeping priced?

Usually a fixed monthly fee based on transaction volume and the services you need, agreed up front.
