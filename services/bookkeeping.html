Are your books always a month behind? FI Consultancy keeps your books accurate, up to date and ready for decisions. Day-to-day bookkeeping: sales, purchases, expenses and payroll recorded accurately. Every bank and card account is reconciled each month, so your balances always agree. A structured month-end close, with accruals and adjustments finished within five working days. We work in QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage and Zoho Books, and connect the apps you already use. Then every month you receive clear reports: profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow. Our approach is simple: onboard, clean up, automate, then close your books every month. The result: accurate records, on-time reports, and more time to run your business.
