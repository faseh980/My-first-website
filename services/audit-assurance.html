Overview

Does this sound familiar?

Reliable financial information is the foundation of every good decision. We keep your records accurate, prepare financial statements that meet IFRS requirements, and get you ready for audit so there are no surprises at year end.

  • Year-end close takes weeks and the numbers still don't reconcile.
  • A bank, investor or regulator is asking for audited financial statements.
  • You're unsure whether your reporting meets IFRS or local requirements.
  • Controls are informal and you worry about errors or fraud.

How we help

Our audit & assurance services

01

Audit preparation & support

We prepare schedules, reconciliations and supporting documents, and work alongside your auditors to keep the audit on time.

02

Bookkeeping & accounting

Accurate monthly bookkeeping, bank reconciliations, payables and receivables, handled by qualified accountants.

03

Financial statement preparation

Annual and interim financial statements prepared under IFRS, IFRS for SMEs or local frameworks.

04

Internal audit & controls review

An independent review of your processes that identifies gaps and recommends practical controls.

05

Agreed-upon procedures

Focused reviews for lenders, grant providers or partners who need specific information verified.

06

Accounting systems & cloud setup

Selection, setup and migration to cloud accounting software, with a clean chart of accounts.
