# 10-Day LinkedIn Content Plan
**Global Accounting • Bookkeeping • Financial Consultancy • Taxation**

Goal: build trust with business owners worldwide, show expertise, and turn followers into booked consultations by Day 10.

**Visual style (all 10 graphics):** deep navy + gold, clean corporate type, 1080×1350 portrait (shows large in the LinkedIn feed).
**Before posting:** open each Canva design, add your **logo, firm name and website**, then *Share → Download → PNG*.
**Every graphic has 4 AI options.** The "Edit" link is the one already saved to your Canva. The other options are listed after each post if you prefer a different look.

## DAY 4: Profit ≠ Cash
**Canva design:** [Edit](https://www.canva.com/d/GptFJ-GlPDR2JkR) · [View](https://www.canva.com/d/n9RhVsDXUKH5iDo)
Other options: [2](https://www.canva.com/d/sRcqrzwGJPQHo_0) · [3](https://www.canva.com/d/8p9m5O9KyqFpuFj) · [4](https://www.canva.com/d/dRke_4nsGlLjfFs)

**Post copy:**
> "But we were profitable… how did we run out of cash?"
>
> We hear this more often than you'd expect. Profit is an opinion. Cash is a fact.
>
> Your P&L can show a healthy profit while your bank account struggles, because:
> • Customers pay you in 60–90 days, but suppliers want paying in 30
> • Inventory soaks up cash before it ever sells
> • Tax bills and loan repayments don't show up as "expenses" the way you'd expect
>
> 3 habits that protect your cash:
> 📈 Forecast 13 weeks ahead and update it weekly
> ⏱️ Shorten your receivables: invoice the same day, automate reminders, offer early-payment discounts
> 🧾 Plan tax payments before they're due and set aside a percentage of every sale
>
> Cash flow forecasting is one of the most valuable things a finance partner can build for you.
>
> ♻️ Repost this for a founder who needs to hear it.
>
> #CashFlow #FinancialPlanning #CFO #BusinessFinance #Startups #SMEs #FinancialConsulting
