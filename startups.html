FAQs

Questions founders ask us

When should a startup hire an accountant?

As early as possible, ideally before your first sale. Getting the structure, tax registrations and bookkeeping right from day one is much cheaper than fixing them later.

Can you help us prepare for fundraising?

Yes. We build an investor-ready financial model, support the valuation, prepare your data room and help you answer investor questions.

We're a small team. Do we need a full-time finance person?

Usually not. Our monthly bookkeeping and fractional CFO services give you the finance support you need at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire.
